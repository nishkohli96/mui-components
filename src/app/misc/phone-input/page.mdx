# MUIPhoneInput

`MUIPhoneInput` wraps [react-international-phone](https://react-international-phone.vercel.app/)
inside a [TextField](https://v7.mui.com/material-ui/react-text-field/), with a searchable country
dropdown. It emits a structured `MUIPhoneInputValue` (`phone`, `country`, `dialCode`, `phoneNo`).

## Playground

Integrated with [Formik](https://formik.org/) — the structured
`MUIPhoneInputValue` with validation on `phoneNo`, plus `preferredCountries`,
`forceDialCode` and a hidden country search.

## Props

`MUIPhoneInputProps` also accepts most [TextFieldProps](https://v7.mui.com/material-ui/api/text-field/) except `value`, `onChange` and `type`. Props marked with <strong style={{ color: 'var(--mui-palette-error-main)' }}>{'*'}</strong> are required.
